# TACMD: Tail-Anchored Counterfactual Medium Disentanglement

Date: 2026-07-31
Branch: `refactor/core-framework`

## Goal

TACMD targets the current failure mode where water-medium parameters and Gaussian clear colors can compensate for each other during underwater RGB training, leaving blue/green residuals in the dewatered `J`.

The module is default-off and keeps the main underwater render path unchanged. It adds:

1. Tail-water evidence and a non-learned scene EMA tail color anchor.
2. Tail-anchored A/B_inf chroma calibration.
3. Depth-adaptive BS log-ratio tolerance band and finite BS convergence losses.
4. A training-only counterfactual BS render whose gradients are routed only to Gaussian color.

## Interpretation

TACMD is useful as a diagnostic and as a color-attribution pressure, but it is not a formal replacement for M1/P3 or the later capacity-support line.

Main findings:

1. `T1 tail` is the best IUI3 reconstruction-safe TACMD variant. It improves PSNR and reduces J Blue by 24.4%, but it does not solve occupancy: water accumulation rises 45.3%, Far Accum only drops 1.9%, and Far Clear only drops 8.9%.
2. `T2 tail+BS` can reduce IUI3 Far Clear and far BG connected residual area, but it strongly increases water/far accumulation. The BS band/convergence losses are not safely disentangling medium from Gaussian capacity.
3. `T3/T4 counterfactual` are not stable as implemented. They can change J chroma or water J, but they also increase accumulation and frequently lose reconstruction quality.
4. JapaneseGradens does not confirm cross-scene stability. T4 is the only variant that improves Far Accum, Far Clear, Water J, and J Blue together, but it loses 0.2167 dB PSNR and leaves water accumulation above M1.

## Decision

Do not continue expanding TACMD T-series as the main experimental line.

Keep:

```text
tacmd tail evidence
scene anchor buffers
T1 tail-anchor calibration as a possible weak auxiliary
TACMD diagnostics for medium/Gaussian compensation
```

Stop or quarantine:

```text
T2 BS band/convergence as active loss
T3/T4 counterfactual projection as active loss
counterfactual loss expansion without an explicit capacity-support mechanism
```

The next useful experiment should not increase TACMD weights. It should return to the capacity-support mechanism and use TACMD only as an optional weak color anchor or diagnostic, because the unresolved failure is still Gaussian occupancy/footprint in far water rather than only B_inf or clear-color chroma.
